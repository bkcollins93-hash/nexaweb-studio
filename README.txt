BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. Open script.js.
2. Find: const NEXAWEB_WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "256XXXXXXXXX";
3. Replace 256XXXXXXXXX with your Uganda WhatsApp number in international format.
   Example format: 2567XXXXXXXX (do not include +, spaces or the leading 0).
4. Review the text and only advertise services you can currently deliver.
5. Keep the sample portfolio concepts labelled as concepts. Do not present them as completed client work.
6. Test on your phone before sharing.

This is a static starter portfolio. It does not process payments, store customer data, or provide a backend.
For commercial client websites, arrange suitable commercial hosting and domain ownership with each client.
